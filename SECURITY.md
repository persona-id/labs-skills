# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security vulnerabilities to **security@withpersona.com**. Do not disclose them publicly (GitHub issues, discussions or pull requests) until we have had a chance to investigate.

Include a description of the issue, steps to reproduce it, the affected skill or file, and your assessment of the impact.

We will acknowledge your report, investigate it with you, and fix confirmed issues based on severity. Eligible reports may qualify for a reward through Persona's bug bounty program, subject to the terms of that program.
